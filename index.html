Why VDR

Fixing your car is about trust.

Most of our new business comes from customers who've already had their car fixed here — and sent us their friends and family.

VDR has been a locally owned shop in Laramie since 1992, run by owner-partners Bob Van De Rostyne and Debbie Rieger. We pride ourselves on customer service and attention to detail, and we work to get your vehicle fixed right the first time.

We also believe in giving back to the community we live in, and we support local charities and University of Wyoming programs. It's part of why customers keep coming back — and keep sending their friends and family our way.
